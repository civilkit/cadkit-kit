0.6.1 11 November 2026

All changes in 0.6.1 (7)
  • Mirroring a sheet metal part keeps it a sheet. With replace the mirror image takes the part's place and unfolds the same, about any plane including a flat face.
  • Scripts can pick edges and faces by position. edges(...) and faces(...) gain .sortBy, .groupBy, .nth, .first and .last, so a script can ask for "the two highest edges", "every edge at height 12" or "the third rung along the rail"…
  • Choose the mass unit and digits. Settings > General > Mass display shows masses in automatic (g or kg), grams, kilograms, pounds or ounces, with 3 to 6 significant digits, in the Inspect panel, the material editor, the bill of…

  • Surface controls for roughness, metalness, clear coat and the wood grain. The Display menu's appearance section gains Roughness, Metalness and Clear coat sliders and, for woods (or when you tick Wood grain), the ring spacing, contrast, colour and direction…

0.6.0 11 November 2026

All changes in 0.6.0 (20)
  • The Washer wizard can put a washer on a hole. Click "Washer on a hole", then a hole's wall or circular edge.
  • An arc slot can be edited after it is drawn. Click one of its arcs in the sketch and change its radius, start angle, sweep or width in the fields that appear.
  • The guide says CivilKit CAD, has the suite navigation, and covers this release. The guide's text now uses the product's name, its menu bar links to Calcs, Calculators, CAD, Guides and What's new, and the new features (BOM as text, Delete both, Enter repeat, woods and grain…
  • A flange or hem can go on the free end of a flange (a double fold). Clicking the edge at the tip of an existing flange now adds a second flange or a hem there; before, the app refused with "edge already has a flange".
  • Woods show a grain. A part with a solid wood material (walnut, oak, birch and the rest) now shows growth rings and fine streaks in a darker tone of its colour, along the longest side of the part.
  • A fold can cross a window in the sheet. A fold line that runs through a hole or window of a sheet metal panel now bends the part and cuts the window through the bend; before, it was refused with "a hole of the panel meets the bend zone".
  • A plain extrude cut through a folded sheet keeps it a sheet. A through-all (or deep enough) Extrude cut drawn parallel to the sheet is now a sheet cut, so the part still unfolds; a shallow pocket still ends the sheet.
  • A sketch on the plate survives removing the only fold. The plate's faces keep working under both of their names, so a slot drawn on the plate no longer loses its reference when you remove the fold.
  • Enter repeats the last command; the view cube's edges and corners are bigger. With nothing in progress (no sketch, no feature being edited, no dialog), Enter runs Repeat last command again.
  • Deleting a feature that others use offers to delete them too. When a delete is blocked, the notice now names the features that use it and has a Delete both button that removes them together in one step (one Undo brings them back).
  • Eight more woods and three wood panels in the Material list. Birch, cedar, cherry, Douglas fir, eucalyptus, maple, teak and walnut join Oak and Pine, each with density and stiffness from a named source and its own colour.
  • The bill of materials copies and saves as plain text. The assembly BOM gains Copy as text and Save as text (aligned columns, ready for an e-mail).
  • The parts library is credited as BOSL2-based parts. The library formerly named "BOSL2 parts" is now BOSL2-based parts, with a credit line (BOSL2 by Revar Desmera and contributors, BSD-2-Clause, link) on its row in the Libraries tab, in each part's…
  • A wheel zoom speed setting. Settings > Navigation gains Wheel zoom speed (0.5x to 3x, 1x as before) for the mouse wheel and trackpad pinch, in the 3D view and in a sketch; it is remembered on the device.
  • An agent can see and drive the part you have open (agent link). Settings gains Agent link: paste the one-time code an MCP assistant gets from bridge_pair and it can read the open part (app_state), take a picture of it (app_screenshot) and, only if you…
  • A Solarized Dark theme. Settings > Theme gains Solarized Dark, a dark theme in the Solarized palette (panels, text, accents and the viewport background); it is remembered like the other themes.
  • OBJ export keeps the body names and colours. Exporting OBJ now writes one object per body, named as in the tree, and a body with a colour carries it on its vertices (x y z r g b, read by Blender, MeshLab and most viewers).
  • A misspelt agent-tool field is refused with a suggestion. A tool call with an unknown field (veiw for view) fails before anything runs, naming the closest allowed field, listing the allowed ones and saying that parameters go at the top level of the…
  • Pipe cut says what it does. Its editor now notes that it rolls a ball along the picked edges and leaves a surface body, and points to Hole (or a sketch and a cut) for a round hole.
  • A Washer wizard for flat and spring washers. Pick a standard size from a filterable list (ISO 7089, ISO 7093-1, DIN 127 B) or type your own, then place it like a screw.

0.5.0 10 November 2026

A Sheet metal tab

Convert a solid to sheet, add edge flanges, hems and corner reliefs, cut across bends and round or chamfer corners. Edge flange, Hem and Corner open with their pick list ready: tap the edges or corners in the model, tap again to take one out.

Flanges on part of an edge, with shaped legs

An edge flange can cover the whole edge, a start and length along it, or the span between two points you pick, with shaped legs, holes and relief. Bases with rounded corners take flanges too.

SolidWorks sheet metal opens as editable sheet metal

Open an SLDPRT sheet metal part and its flanges come back as editable Edge flange features, with partial-width and sketched-profile flanges, leg rounds, slots and holes.

Arc slot

A curved slot in the sketch tools: pick the centre, the start of the slot and its end, then set the radius and width.

Dimension to the part's own edge

With the Dimension tool, click an outer edge of the part to set how far a slot or hole sits from it.

Library

Samples is now Library: every library in one picker, a Manage libraries list, pictures on saved parts, and Insert into this part on each card.
