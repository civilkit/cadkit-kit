# Turbo impeller

The **Impeller** command (Features tab, next to Propeller) builds an open-type radial compressor
wheel: a contoured hub, N back-swept main blades plus N shorter splitter blades, and a shaft
bore. It is a standalone wizard (Hub, Blade, Review), not a family inside the Propeller wizard —
the parameter model is kept neutral so a radial-inflow turbine variant can share it later.

## How it is built

The route is the one the kernel probe proved (the brief's work order 1): everything a loft of
closed sections in planes through the shaft axis can do, and nothing thinner.

1. **Hub** — a sketch on an *angle* plane (u = world Z, v = the radial direction at 0) holding the
   meridional profile (nose face, cone to a knee at 75% of the length, tip edge, back face, and
   the closing edge on the axis). Revolved 360° about that closing edge.
2. **One band section per station** (10–16 by the brief) — each on its own angle plane at
   psi + 90, where psi is the blade wrap at that station. Blade thickness is *tangential*
   (perpendicular to the section plane), so the section is the thin band where the plane cuts the
   blade: the hub-to-shroud line shifted ±Δm **along the meridional direction**, with
   Δm = (t/2) / (r dθ/dm) in m-parameter units, scaled by the meridional speed |dH/dm| into
   millimetres. The root end of the line sits `rootEmbed` below the hub surface so the fuse holds;
   the marine family's lesson (a sheet end tangent to the hub surface makes the boolean fail) is
   what the embed avoids.
3. **Blade** — the sections lofted (add), then circular-patterned about z (mainBlades copies).
4. **Splitters** — the same sections from `splitterStart` onward, lofted and patterned
   `splitterBlades` times, phased half a pitch between the mains.
5. **Bore** — a circle on the front plane, extruded cut through both (skipped when the bore
   diameter is 0).

Two probe lessons are baked in: shifting the section line *along itself* (instead of along the
meridional tangent) produces zero-area quads the kernel refuses as `loftProfile`; and the
parameter-space Δm must be scaled by |dH/dm| or the bands are ~18× too thin to loft into a real
blade. The default (7 + 7 blades, 45 mm exducer, 75° wrap, 12 stations) builds in about 5–7 s as
one valid solid.

## Parameters

**Hub step** — exducer and inducer diameters, hub length, bore (0 = none), main blade count
(3–12), splitter count (0 up to the main count).

**Blade step** — total wrap (20–160°, backsweep for a compressor), tangential thickness at the
hub end and at the shroud end of the section line, splitter start (a fraction of the meridional
span), the axial and radial reach of the hub-to-shroud line, the root embed, and the section
count. The review step reports the radii, counts, wrap, the section line length and the tip
pitch, and lists issues: errors block Create, warnings do not. If a preview fails, the wizard
retries with 12, 10 and then 14 sections (the marine family's spacing retry, in stations) and
says which one built.

Reopening: click any feature the impeller made and the wizard comes back on its parameters, like
the marine family's. The record lives on the hub sketch (`family: "impeller"`).

## Limits

One meridional blade shape only — no leading-edge sweep, no varying wrap along the span, no
shroud surface (the section lines simply end where the shroud would be). Blades fuse to the hub
without a root fillet. The knee of the hub contour is fixed at 75% of the length.
