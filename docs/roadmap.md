# Roadmap

Gaps and planned larger work, newest first. Small items live in the issue tracker; this page names the big ones. Status values: proposed,
specified, in progress, done.

| Item | Status | Spec | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motion studies: drive a mate through its range, find where and why it stops (slot ends, contact), play and export it | done (`0.8.6`) | `docs/spec-motion-studies.md` | [#69](https://github.com/civilkit/cadkit-kit/issues/69) |

Why motion is first: assemblies can be posed but not moved, so a slot, hinge, drawer or linkage cannot be tested for its limits without
editing numbers by hand.
